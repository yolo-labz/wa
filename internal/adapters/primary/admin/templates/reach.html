Unsupported address

The admin UI is served only to a loopback origin or to an HTTPS origin listed in WAD_ADMIN_HTTPS_ORIGINS. Reach it in one of two ways:

  • host-native daemon: tailscale serve (never funnel) in front of WAD_ADMIN_ADDR, with that name in WAD_ADMIN_HTTPS_ORIGINS;
  • container: a local forwarder bound to 127.0.0.1 whose per-connection command runs wad admin-tunnel inside the container, then open localhost on the forwarder's port.
